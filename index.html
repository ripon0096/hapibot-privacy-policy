This Privacy Policy applies to the MEMORY (HapiBot) mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") developed by Ripon Chowdhury. This document explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our Application.

1. Prominent Disclosure: Accessibility Service Usage

⚠️ AccessibilityService API Declaration & Compliance

The Application uses Android's AccessibilityService API (specifically AutoClickService) to provide smart touch automation and assist users with automated repetitive actions in designated supported applications.

  • Purpose of Use: The Accessibility Service is strictly used to perform authorized automated tap, click, and gesture interactions initiated and scheduled by the user.
  • No Keystroke or Password Logging: The Accessibility Service NEVER captures, tracks, or records personal keystrokes, passwords, financial details, PINs, or private chat messages.
  • No Unauthorized Audio/Video/Screen Recording: The service does not perform audio recording, phone call capture, or background screen surveillance.
  • User Consent & Control: The Accessibility Service is completely optional. It requires explicit user activation from Android System Settings (Accessibility) and can be enabled, paused, or revoked by the user at any time.

2. Permissions and Data We Collect

To provide core automation and verification features, the Application requests specific permissions as outlined below:

Permission Technical Identifier Purpose & Usage
Accessibility Service BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE Performs simulated screen gestures and taps for assisted task automation when activated by the user.
Location Access ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Used for region-specific service availability, network latency optimization, and device validation. Location data is NOT stored or sold.
Package Visibility QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Checks whether compatible target applications (such as Hapi or Masti) are installed on the device so the automation service can interface with them.
Foreground Service & Notifications FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays an ongoing notification while the automation service is active, ensuring user awareness and transparent background state.
Internet Access INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to check network connectivity, authenticate account licenses, and fetch service configurations.
System Alert Window SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Displays a floating overlay control button (Start/Stop) on top of the screen for direct user control.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We respect your privacy. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties or advertising networks.

The Application may communicate with secure backend infrastructure (such as Firebase/Cloudflare) solely for:

  • Verifying activation licenses and user subscriptions.
  • Receiving configuration parameters for app updates and security definitions.
  • Diagnostic error logging to maintain stability and prevent crashes.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards to protect any operational settings and data transmitted by the Application. No sensitive personal credentials, chat histories, or credit card numbers are collected, transmitted, or stored on our servers.

5. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your device and data:

  • Revoke Permissions: You can disable any permission (Accessibility, Location, Notifications) at any time through your device's Settings > Apps > MEMORY.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all local caches, databases, and preferences stored on your device.
  • Request Account / Data Removal: If you wish to request the deletion of any associated account identifiers or license data, you can contact us directly via email.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Continued use of the Application signifies acceptance of the updated policy.
